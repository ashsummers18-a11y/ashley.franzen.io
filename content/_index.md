Ashley N. Franzen is a freelance writer, editor, photographer, and educator based in Bonn, Germany. She is a lifelong nomad and writer, specializing on topics in science & environment, travel & culture, education & parenting, health & wellness, food & beverage.

She competently crafts and innovates with words, using creative writing techniques to shape and evolve the narrative and share your story.

Her work has been published by _University&nbsp;of&nbsp;Copenhagen_, _Fine&nbsp;Finish_, _BlueandGreenTomorrow.com_, _BrainsBeautyandBabies.com_ and other publications.

Ashley N. Franzen is a freelance writer, editor, photographer, and educator based in Bonn, Germany. She is a lifelong nomad and writer, specializing on topics in sustainability, health and wellness, family and parenting, international living, lifestyle, travel, and beer.

Her work has been published by _University of Copenhagen_, _Fine Finish_, _BlueandGreenTomorrow.com_, _BrainsBeautyandBabies.com_ and other publications.

For more information, feel free to take a look at my [portfolio](/portfolio).
